CV PDF export uses these vendored UMD builds (no html2pdf — it clones DOM and changes layout).

1) html2canvas 1.4.1
   https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/html2canvas/1.4.1/html2canvas.min.js
   Captures the live .page element in-place (WYSIWYG vs on-screen layout).

2) jsPDF 2.5.1 UMD
   https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/jspdf/2.5.1/jspdf.umd.min.js
   Slices the tall canvas into A4-height strips and saves one multi-page PDF.

To refresh:
  curl -fsSL -o assets/html2canvas.min.js "https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/html2canvas/1.4.1/html2canvas.min.js"
  curl -fsSL -o assets/jspdf.umd.min.js "https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/jspdf/2.5.1/jspdf.umd.min.js"
